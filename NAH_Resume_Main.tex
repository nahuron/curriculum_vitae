\csection{Professional Summary}
\hrule
\vspace{0.75ex}

Senior Data Scientist with a Ph.D. in Biology and 4+ years building production predictive models, statistical analyses, and reproducible analytics pipelines that turn large, messy relational datasets into measurable business decisions. Advanced R developer — including published R package authorship — with working Python and SQL, skilled in machine learning, feature engineering, time-series and rare-event modeling, and model validation. Equally comfortable validating raw database exports and presenting model-driven strategy to non-technical executives. Published, grant-winning researcher recognized for predictive modeling that quantified real-world economic impact.
